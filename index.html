Alignment Rule

Use the official BC curriculum pages while planning. Paste the exact Big Ideas, Curricular Competencies, and Content you will teach, then include the source URL and access date.

The exported document includes a source section so future readers can verify the plan against the BC curricular documents.

Planning Workspace
